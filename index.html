// Frequently Avoided Questions

FAQ

Can my Mascot do some light work? Just a little?

No. Credentialed units are calibrated for presence, not production. Light work is how it starts. Next thing you know they're "owning a workstream," and you've lost six months and a sales VP.

Isn't this just fraud with a nicer font?

Every credential is 100% real. Every degree was earned. Every FAANG badge was worn. We simply sell them in the way the market already buys them. If that feels like fraud, please take it up with the market.

What if an investor asks the Mascot a technical question?

All units are factory-trained on four universal responses: "It's a great question," "We're thinking about that at the platform level," "Let me loop in the team," and a long, meaningful pause.

Do Mascots require equity?

Yes. More than your first engineer. Less than your landlord. Vesting is immediate, because they're done the moment they appear on the website.

What happens when the company fails?

The Mascot is automatically re-deployed to your nearest competitor with an updated bio: "Previously helped scale [YOUR COMPANY]." Nothing is ever lost. Only recycled.
